Delivery Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

In short: nothing is shipped. Your books arrive as downloadable PDF files, by email, usually within a few minutes of payment.

How delivery works

  1. Pay on the secure Razorpay checkout (UPI, card or net banking).
  2. We prepare your personal copy of the books.
  3. We email a personal download link to the address you entered at checkout, from orders@garbhamantra.com.

Delivery time

Most orders arrive within a few minutes. In rare cases it can take up to 24 hours. If nothing has arrived after 24 hours, check your spam and Promotions folders, then write to hello@garbhamantra.com and we will send it again.

Your download link

  • The link is personal to your order. Please don't share it.
  • It works for 30 days, with up to 5 downloads of each book.
  • Once you've saved the files, they're yours to keep and use for as long as you like. If your link expires or you lose the files, email us and we'll send a fresh link.

Your personal copy

Every page of your copy carries a small line showing the email address and order number it was licensed to. This keeps your copy personal and helps us protect the work of the people who made it.

Shipping charges

None. There are no physical goods and no delivery fees.

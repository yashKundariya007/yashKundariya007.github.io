Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

In short: because the books are digital downloads delivered instantly, we don't offer refunds once an order is placed. If a file is broken, missing or never reaches you, we will fix it or send it again, free.

No refunds on digital downloads

Garbhamantra books are PDF files sent by email immediately after payment. A downloaded file can't be returned, so all sales are final and orders can't be cancelled once payment is complete. Please read the book descriptions, page counts and sample pages on our website before you buy.

What we will always do

  • Broken or damaged file: if a PDF won't open, has missing or unreadable pages, or doesn't match what the website describes, we will fix it and send you a working copy.
  • Email didn't arrive: if you haven't received your download email within 24 hours (please check your spam and Promotions folders first), we will send it again.
  • Download link expired: we will send you a fresh link.
  • Charged twice for the same order: we will refund the duplicate payment in full to the original payment method. Refunds usually reach your account within 5–7 working days.
  • Money deducted but payment failed: these are normally reversed automatically by your bank or Razorpay within 5–7 working days. If it hasn't been reversed after that, write to us and we will help.

How to ask for help

Email hello@garbhamantra.com from the address you used at checkout, with your order number (it's in your order email) and a short description of the problem. We reply within one working day.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Garbhamantra (a brand of Rayherb Ayurveda, the data fiduciary) collects, why, and what you can do about it. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • When you buy: your name, email address, phone number, what you ordered, the amount paid and the Razorpay payment reference.
  • When you download: the time of each download and which book, so we can apply the download limit.
  • When you email us: your email address and whatever you choose to write.

We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay handles these directly.

Why we use it

  • to deliver your books and print your licence line (email and order number) on each page;
  • to send your order and download emails;
  • to help you if something goes wrong with an order;
  • to keep the accounting records the law requires.

We don't sell your data, and we don't send you marketing emails unless you've asked for them.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the shop for us, and only what each one needs:

  • Razorpay: payments;
  • Supabase: our order database and file storage;
  • Hostinger Email: our inbox and order emails;
  • GitHub Pages: hosting this website.

Some of these providers store data outside India. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. It saves one small value in your browser (gm_bonus_start) to remember when your 90-minute bonus window started. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires, usually up to 8 years. Download logs are kept for 12 months. Emails to our inbox are kept for as long as they're useful for helping you.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it (except records we must keep by law). You can also raise a complaint. Write to our grievance contact, Yash Kundariya, at hello@garbhamantra.com. We will reply within 7 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
